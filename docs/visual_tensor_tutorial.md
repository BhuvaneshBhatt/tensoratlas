# Visual tensor tutorial notes

The main tutorial notebook includes visualization cells that complement the symbolic examples. These figures are generated by `tensoratlas.examples.visualizations` and are optional: they require the `plot` dependency group.

The visualizations cover:

1. basis changes and component changes;
2. covariant and contravariant scaling;
3. covectors as level-set families;
4. dual bases and the Kronecker-delta pairing;
5. metric unit ellipses;
6. raising and lowering as the metric-induced vector-to-covector map;
7. tensor products as outer-product heatmaps;
8. contractions as slot-wiring diagrams;
9. coordinate-basis variation in vector fields;
10. 1-forms and 2-forms;
11. pullbacks as mapped grids;
12. curvature on a sphere;
13. geodesics on a sphere;
14. stress tensors on material elements;
15. strain deformations;
16. quadrupole density patterns;
17. geometric-algebra rotor rotations;
18. canonicalization as convergence to a normal form.

These examples are deliberately small. They are meant to clarify the meaning of tensor components, slots, transformations, metrics, forms, curvature, and canonicalization before users move into heavier symbolic workflows.
